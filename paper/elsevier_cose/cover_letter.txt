Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our original research manuscript titled "SonarMoE: Remote Prompt and Tool-Schema Reconstruction via Expert Contention in Disaggregated MoE Serving and Differentially Private Mitigation" for consideration for publication in Computers & Security.

Production Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) serving deployments increasingly adopt Attention-FFN Disaggregation (AFD), isolating tenant Attention containers and Key-Value (KV) caches while sharing an Expert-Parallel feed-forward GPU pool. Although AFD neutralizes shared-KV prefix-cache attacks and host co-residency attacks, we demonstrate that an unprivileged remote HTTP streaming client can still infer co-scheduled victim prompt domains and Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool schemas solely from client-side Inter-Token Latency (ITL) timestamps.

Our main contributions are:
1. Tile-Boundary Side-Channel Capacity Theory (Theorem 1): We show that sizing public offline-synthesized canary micro-batches just below discrete GPU matrix-multiply tile cliffs (N_can = M_tile - delta) amplifies single-probe mutual information by 2.9x-17.8x over uncalibrated probing under network jitter.
2. Blind Attack Evaluation under Zero Length Leakage (MoELeak-Bench): Using single-GPU Apple M5 Pro FP16 kernel profiling, hardware-grounded AFD simulation across synthetic jitter (10-200 us), and live localhost HTTP/1.1 SSE loopback verification on OLMoE-1B-7B and Granite-3.1-MoE with strict constant-length windows (B_vic = 64 tokens, 0.00 bits length leakage), SonarMoE achieves up to 87.0% five-domain accuracy and 90.0% Top-1 (94.4% Top-3) accuracy across 20 enterprise MCP tool schemas.
3. Sound Per-Tenant Differentially Private Mitigation (OblivDispatch, Theorem 2): We design a strictly per-tenant three-stage dispatcher combining shadow-expert overflow inside cross-rank barrier slack with a shifted two-sided Gaussian mechanism, data-independent clipping [N_min, N_max], and split failure accounting (delta = delta_zCDP + delta_tail). Across all evaluated budgets and seeds, OblivDispatch achieves zero observed underflow slots, drives attacker accuracy to the random-guess floor, obeys our analytical rho-zCDP mutual information upper bound, and keeps serving TPOT overhead at +2.33% to +2.42% on Granite-3.1-MoE and +7.94% to +8.93% on OLMoE-1B-7B.
